Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal, located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, is a symbol of eternal love and one of the world's most famous monuments. It is renowned for its white marble architecture and nearby attractions like the Main Mausoleum, Charbagh Garden, Mehtab Bagh, and Agra Fort.

  • Main Mausoleum
  • Charbagh Garden
  • Mehtab Bagh
  • Agra Fort

Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace, located in Mysuru, Karnataka, is one of India's most magnificent royal palaces. It is renowned for its beautiful Indo-Saracenic architecture and attractions such as the Durbar Hall, Kalyana Mantapa, Palace Museum, and Mysore Zoo.

  • Durbar Hall
  • Kalyana Mantapa
  • Palace Museum
  • Mysore Zoo

Varanasi Temple

Varanasi, located on the banks of the River Ganga in Uttar Pradesh, is one of the world's oldest cities. It is famous for the Kashi Vishwanath Temple and other attractions like Dashashwamedh Ghat, Sarnath, and Assi Ghat.

  • Kashi Vishwanath Temple
  • Dashashwamedh Ghat
  • Sarnath
  • Assi Ghat
